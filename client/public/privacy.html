Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026

Twingle ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information we collect

  • Account info: name, email, password (hashed).
  • Location: precise location used to discover nearby users.

How we use it

  • To provide nearby discovery and chat features.

  • To send push notifications (if enabled).

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only as needed to operate the service (e.g., with hosting providers).

Data retention

You can delete your account at any time; we remove associated data.
